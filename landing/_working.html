Questions we get asked

Does Finance Audit Pro process payments?

No. It records collections you have already received. No payment gateway, no bank verification.

Does Finance Audit Pro support Telugu?

Yes. The app runs in English and Telugu with the same customer and ledger records.

How are amounts stored in Finance Audit Pro?

As whole paise. Nothing is rounded to a rupee, so totals stay exact.

Finance Audit Pro: add multiple users

Yes. Add team members to view collections, ledgers and exports. All users see the same records in their chosen language.

Dues and collections in Finance Audit Pro

Dues are what customers owe for the week. Collections are what you have collected. Each collection posts to the ledger and weekly tracker.

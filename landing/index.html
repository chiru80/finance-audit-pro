Questions we get asked

Straight answers for finance teams evaluating Finance Audit Pro.

Q: Is my data stored in paise or rupees?
Answer
✓All amounts are stored as integer paise (₹1 = 100 paise). This eliminates floating-point errors and ensures audit-grade precision. The UI displays rupees with two decimals for readability.
Q: Can I export data for my auditor?
Answer
✓Yes. Weekly summaries export as CSV and PDF with immutable audit trails — timestamps, user IDs, and entry hashes included. No manual compilation needed.
Q: What payment methods are supported?
Answer
✓Cash and UPI. Each collection records the method, reference number (optional for UPI), and timestamp. The ledger splits totals by method automatically.
Q: How does the weekly cycle work?
Answer
✓Weeks run Monday–Sunday. On Monday, overdue items carry forward automatically. You collect Tue–Fri, then generate the weekly summary on the weekend. The cycle repeats.

Export, settings & pricing

What you need to know before you start.

Q: What export formats are available?
Answer
✓CSV for accounting software import, PDF for auditor packets. Both include the immutable audit trail: entry hash, timestamp, user, and method.
Q: Can I customize the week start day?
Answer
✓Yes. Settings let you choose Monday, Saturday, or Sunday as week start. All reports, carry-forwards, and summaries respect your choice.
